### Quick Fixes (`Ctrl+.`) & Custom Thresholds

- Press **`Ctrl+.`** (or click the lightbulb 💡) on any Big-O diagnostic to insert `// bigo-ignore` above an intentional hotspot or jump straight to the Dashboard.
- Customize when `🔥` triggers (`O(n log n)`, `O(n²)`, or `O(2^n)`) and diagnostic severity (`Hint`, `Information`, `Warning`).

[Open Big-O Settings](command:workbench.action.openSettings?%22bigoAnalyzer%22)
