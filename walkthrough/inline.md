### See Time & Space Complexity Inline

Open any `.ts`, `.tsx`, `.js`, or `.jsx` file to immediately see:

- **CodeLens badges** (`⏱️ Time: O(n) | 💾 Space: O(1)` or `🔥` for hotspots) above every function, method, and arrow function—click to jump straight to the bottleneck line.
- **Hover tooltips** showing **total vs. self** complexity, line-by-line construct reasons, and concrete optimization tips.
- **Worst-bottleneck line highlight** and **Status Bar** summary at the bottom-right.

Try it right away on [sample/demo.ts](sample/demo.ts) or open any TypeScript/JavaScript file in your project.
